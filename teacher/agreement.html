Teacher Agreement and Online Teaching Code of Conduct

"Empowering Minds, Transforming Futures."

1. Purpose and Scope

This Agreement establishes the terms and conditions governing your engagement by CKH International School and sets out the professional, academic, safeguarding, confidentiality and online teaching standards you agree to follow.

2–4. Appointment, Responsibilities & Online Delivery

You agree to prepare and deliver high-quality online lessons, prepare resources, attend lessons punctually, maintain attendance records, assess and give feedback, monitor student progress, communicate professionally through authorised channels, maintain academic records, attend required meetings/training, and report safeguarding, behavioural, academic or technical concerns. All official lessons are conducted through the official CKH platform or another platform expressly authorised by the School.

5–6. Student Communication & No Private Meetings

You must not privately communicate with any CKH student outside the official CKH platform or authorised School channels — this includes personal WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, personal email, personal phone numbers, or any other private messaging app. You must not arrange private one-to-one meetings with students outside the approved platform, or independently offer paid private tutoring without written School approval.

7–8. Safeguarding & Professional Boundaries

You will maintain professional boundaries, treat students with dignity, report safeguarding concerns immediately, and never engage in inappropriate, sexual, romantic, grooming, threatening, discriminatory, or secretive conduct with students. You must not develop inappropriate personal relationships with students, flirt, engage in romantic or sexual communication, send inappropriate content, discuss inappropriate personal matters, encourage secrecy, or give personal gifts without School approval.

9–10. Camera, Environment & Recording

You will maintain a professional appearance and environment, appropriate background, suitable lighting/sound, and minimise distractions. Where lessons are recorded, recordings may be used for legitimate educational, safeguarding, quality-assurance, training, or administrative purposes. You will not independently record or distribute lessons without authorisation.

11–12. Confidentiality & Student Data

You will maintain strict confidentiality of student, parent, staff, financial, academic, business, technology, security, and internal School information. Student information may only be collected, accessed, stored, or used for legitimate educational purposes authorised by the School. Suspected data breaches must be reported immediately.

13–17. Academic Standards, Attendance & Assessment

You will follow the approved curriculum, prepare lessons in advance, attend all assigned lessons (notifying the School as soon as reasonably possible if unable to teach), assess fairly, and never alter marks dishonestly, provide unauthorised examination answers, or manipulate results. AI tools may be used only in accordance with School guidelines and must never misrepresent another person's work as your own.

18–21. Intellectual Property, Branding & Private Business

Teaching materials created specifically for CKH as part of your duties are subject to the agreed intellectual-property arrangements. You will protect the School's reputation and not use its name, logo, or branding for personal commercial purposes without authorisation, and will not solicit CKH students for private tutoring, paid consultancy, or other commercial services without written School approval.

22–26. Communication, Technology & Performance

Parent/guardian communication is conducted through official School channels. You will maintain access to suitable technology for online teaching, keep passwords confidential, report suspicious activity, and participate in required staff meetings and training. The School may monitor teaching quality through lesson observations, feedback, attendance records, and academic reviews.

27–29. Payment, Expenses & Conflicts of Interest

Payment is made according to the agreed remuneration arrangement communicated to you separately (payment structure, rate, and frequency). Expenses incurred on behalf of CKH require prior approval where required. You must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest.

30–34. Conduct, Discipline & Termination

Professional conduct is required at all times. Misconduct may include harassment, bullying, discrimination, fraud, theft, confidentiality breaches, unauthorised student contact, safeguarding violations, or conduct that seriously damages the School's reputation. Depending on circumstances, breaches may result in a warning, mandatory training, suspension, restricted platform access, termination, or referral to relevant authorities. Following termination, you must not use confidential School information or systems to contact students for personal, commercial, or unauthorised purposes.

35–38. Governing Law, Disputes & Amendments

This Agreement complies with applicable laws concerning online education, employment/services, child protection, safeguarding, privacy, and data protection. The Parties will first attempt good-faith discussion to resolve disputes, and any amendment must be made in writing and approved by authorised representatives.

39. Teacher Declaration

By signing below, you confirm that you have read and understood this Agreement, agree to follow School policies, understand safeguarding and professional-boundary requirements, agree not to communicate privately with CKH students outside authorised School channels, agree not to solicit students for unauthorised private services, and agree to protect student and School information.

Appendix B — Non-Negotiable Student Communication Rule

All student-teacher communication must remain within the official CKH platform or an officially authorised School communication channel. You are strictly prohibited from establishing private communication channels with CKH students. A breach may constitute serious misconduct and may result in disciplinary action, suspension, or termination, subject to applicable law.
