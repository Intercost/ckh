CKH International School Terms & Conditions

Please review the rules below, then sign to submit your application.

1 Show up, be honest, do your own work ▾
  • Attend all scheduled lessons and complete required activities on time.
  • Follow teacher instructions and keep your account information accurate.
  • Submit only your own work — no cheating, plagiarism, unauthorised collusion, or impersonating another student.
  • Never share assessment answers or falsify academic records.
  • AI tools may only be used when a teacher or the assessment instructions permit it, and never to pass off work as your own.
  • Follow all examination rules, including identity verification, time limits, and any online-proctoring requirements.
Ref: Sections 2, 5, 6, 7 — Student Responsibilities, Attendance & Punctuality, Academic Integrity, Assessments & Examinations
2 Be respectful — no bullying, harassment, or discrimination ▾
  • Join lessons on time, dress appropriately, and use a quiet, suitable learning environment.
  • Mute your microphone when not speaking and use chat appropriately.
  • Cameras may be required during selected lessons or assessments where reasonably necessary.
  • Communicate respectfully with classmates and staff at all times.
  • Bullying, cyberbullying, harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, hate speech, sexual harassment, impersonation, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are all strictly prohibited.
Ref: Sections 3, 4 — Online Classroom Etiquette, Respect, Inclusion & Anti-Bullying
3 Use school systems responsibly and protect privacy ▾
  • School systems are for lawful educational purposes only.
  • Do not attempt unauthorised access, bypass security controls, or share your password.
  • Never install malicious software, upload harmful files, or disrupt school services.
  • Do not misuse another person's account.
  • Respect classmates' and staff's privacy — no recording, screenshotting, photographing, or distributing lessons, chats, or personal information without authorisation.
  • Use only your own assigned account, and report a suspected account compromise immediately.
Ref: Sections 8, 9, 14 — Technology & Acceptable Use, Privacy, Recording & Digital Content, Student Accounts & Security
4 Communicate only through official channels ▾
  • Use official school communication channels for anything related to CKH.
  • Keep messages courteous, relevant, and sent at reasonable times.
  • Do not contact teachers through personal social media or other unauthorised channels for school matters.
Ref: Section 10 — Communication
5 Speak up if something's wrong, and follow the process if you don't agree ▾
  • Report any safeguarding concern immediately — abuse, exploitation, grooming, self-harm, threats, or inappropriate adult contact — through the school's designated process.
  • Depending on seriousness, breaking the rules may lead to a reminder, warning, parent/guardian meeting, restricted platform privileges, removal from a live session, academic sanctions, suspension, or termination of enrolment.
  • You and your parent/guardian may raise concerns through the school's official complaints process.
  • Academic or disciplinary decisions may be appealed where the relevant procedure permits.
  • Retaliation against anyone who raises a genuine concern is not permitted.
Ref: Sections 11, 15, 16 — Safeguarding & Student Welfare, Misconduct & Consequences, Complaints & Appeals
